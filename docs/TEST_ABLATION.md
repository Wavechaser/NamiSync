# Test Ablation Study

Baseline: `cab2259`. This is a diagnostic study of maintenance constraints and
marginal failure detection, not authorization to permanently delete tests or
change product/acceptance policy. Experimental variants stay in ignored copies.

## Closed study register

| ID | Accepted outcome | Named verification | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TA-1 | Census the tracked test/support/oracle corpus and identify structural deletion candidates | Git/AST inventory, full collection, referenced baseline gates, independent reviews | complete |
| TA-2 | Evaluate six finite ablation cohorts selected from the census | Exact predeclared membership, paired control/allowed-variation/causal-fault probes, removed versus retained detector incidence, combined-ablation check; explicit untested losses | complete |
| TA-3 | Rank removal, consolidation, policy-change and retention options | Complete closure cost, overlap/interaction limitations, independent adversarial review, source/protected identity reconciliation | complete |

TA-1 selects six high-information cohorts across source/AST locks, private
representation, database mechanisms, browser/harness layers and protected
settlement evidence. Record exact experiments before TA-2 execution. Each gets
at least one finite variation or fault experiment; failure/infeasibility is an
observation, never equivalence. Discovery findings do not add checkpoints or
authorize product fixes. Repository hard-stop rules remain active.

## Evidence rules

Green deletion establishes only that the remaining selection runs. Measure
marginal detection against named faults and acceptance of behavior-preserving
variations. Unique lost detection is a cost. Preserve public compatibility,
mutation safety, truthful outcomes and independent authority in ordinary-cleanup
recommendations; separately expose policy decisions needed for larger removals.
Counts/timings are diagnostic under DEFENSE section 7. Finite probes cannot prove
general equivalence, runtime complexity, exhaustive coverage or safety. Credit
causal runtime failures, never import/fixture errors or unrelated exceptions.
No permanent test framework, production seam, skip or policy weakening is added.

## Frozen cohorts and procedure

`cohorts.json` freezes exact function membership; `experiment-manifest.json`
freezes control/variation/fault experiments and retained neighborhoods before
execution. Each variant runs its whole neighborhood and again with the cohort
deselected, recording node outcomes. The combined experiment removes all six
cohorts from ordinary selection. This is selection ablation, not a claim that
orphaned support is already deleted; source closure cost is reported separately.
The F experiment also runs the real oracle CLI, distinct from ordinary self-tests.
Failed observations remain append-only.

## Results and recommendations

The next useful reduction is to remove implementation prescriptions and repeated
certification machinery, not merely combine parameter rows. These have different
costs: removing a spelling assertion can free a refactor; retiring an independent
policy oracle gives up a distinct form of evidence. This study recommends the
former first and exposes the latter as an explicit policy decision.

The census covers all 168 tracked test/support files plus the two oracle files
within that total: 229,113 lines and 4,674 collected cases. This includes reference
data, not just executable tests. The six cohorts contain 98 test functions,
138 collected cases, and 4,404 lines of selected functions including decorators.
Those figures describe the experimental deletion, not an approved saving.

| Cohort | Selected cases / function lines | Variation failures, full → ablated | Seeded-fault failures, full → ablated | Disposition |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| A: exact FailureDetail source owners | 2 / 127 | 1 → 0 | 24 → 24 | Best small removal candidate: retire exact call-site inventory and its custom AST analyzer; keep runtime lifetime tests. Explicit loss: inventory of newly introduced construction sites. |
| B: SQL shape and batch counts | 5 / 253 | 4 → 2 | 0 → 0 | Consolidate around result correctness, snapshot consistency and bounded work. The initial fault is masked; no redundancy claim. Relaxing the documented 400-item mechanism is a separate decision. |
| C: private integrity index | 15 / 204 | 12 → 0 | 6 → 0 | Split behavioral assertions from private representation checks. Whole-cohort deletion hides the duplicate-guard mutation; observed failures are lookup-mechanism assertions. |
| D: visual declaration mirrors | 11 / 1,187 | 1 → 0 | 1 → 0 | Remove lexical implementation locks; consolidate accessibility and semantic rendering requirements. Keep a reduced-motion detector before deleting the cohort. |
| E: tests of headed-harness spelling | 10 / 412 | 1 → 0 | 2 → 2 | Strong consolidation candidate: replace source-text expectations with existing observable harness checks where they actually cover the obligation. Whole-cohort equivalence is unproved. |

F has one incidental IndexError among its five full fault failures; the other
four are causal assertions (two audit projections and two ordinary settlement
assertions). Its ablated two failures are both causal. C variation failures are
private-name AttributeError/AST-search StopIteration failures, evidence of test
coupling rather than product regressions. No fixture/import errors occurred.

### What each experiment actually established

A renames a private session helper without changing its implementation. The exact
owner inventory rejects that rename. Making graph retirement a no-op remains
caught by 24 behavioral tests after ablation. This does not show that those tests
cover every newly added FailureDetail constructor; the removed inventory uniquely
flags structural drift. Its 118-line AST analyzer and imports become unnecessary
with the two tests (245 function/helper lines before import/blank-line cleanup).
The recommendation deliberately trades that exact inventory for behavior-focused
review, rather than calling the inventory semantically equivalent to runtime tests.

B changes the repository batch size from 400 to 512. Two surviving snapshot tests
still assert the exact number of queries, so deleting the selected shape tests
does not free that mechanism. Preserve tests that put an intervening commit across
a batch boundary, but express the boundary from the chosen batching mechanism
rather than duplicating a literal count. Exact result ordering, relevant aliases,
stale identities, admission limits and one read snapshot remain requirements.
The initial source-identity-filter fault passed its full neighborhood because its fixture
also excluded the same row by target identity. This observation cannot support
removal or establish production equivalence. Query complexity/performance at 512
was not established, and DATABASE currently documents 400.

C renames only the private index while leaving its public property intact. The
remaining neighborhood tolerates that rename, but deleting duplicate-completion
rejection becomes invisible after ablation. All six full-neighborhood failures stop at
the lookup-count assertion before the later duplicate-rejection assertion; they
are mechanism detections, not observed direct duplicate-behavior failures. The cohort also checks detached
candidate snapshots, immutable completion snapshots, unknown IDs and derived-index
replacement/deletion. Preserve these behavioral obligations in ordinary tests;
remove or rewrite the source scan and private-name access separately. VERIFIER
currently promises a shared exact frozenset without copy/rescan, so replacing that
representation needs a policy/documentation decision, not just test deletion.

D adds a nonexecuting JavaScript comment containing “animation”; a source regex
rejects it. Removing the reduced-motion media query in both CSS files is detected
only by an ablated test in the ordinary neighborhood. Retain the accessibility
requirement while dropping bans on mere source words and repeated exact CSS
spelling. Four design-token tests were deliberately retained: pinned transcription,
raw-color ownership/scanner and fixed icon foundation. No real browser/headed run
was performed in this study, so existing gallery tests are potential replacement
owners, not experimentally certified substitutes for every static assertion.

E changes Python string quotation only; AST equality was checked before execution.
A harness source-count assertion still rejects it. Leaking raw exception messages
remains caught by two retained sanitizer tests. That establishes sanitization
coverage only: removed guards also address production-stack fidelity, bounded
probes, exact media/scenario matrices and native picker fail-closed behavior.
Some have retained behavioral tests, but this study did not fault each obligation.
Keep the harness and its behavioral protocol tests; remove the second spelling
specification where the behavior already has an adequate owner. Do not replace
these checks with another generic source-analysis framework.

F adds a report field without changing product execution. Ordinary self-tests
accept it, but the actual frozen-baseline diff rejects it. That is an intentional
trace-authority constraint, not a harmless-change false positive. Suppressing
final recorder-flush errors is caught by retained settlement tests and the real
oracle. Those two observations do not cover the audit's independent scenario
manifest, protected pin, schema checks, no-clobber behavior or other settlement
policy decisions. Real oracle commands used repeat 1 as a diagnostic; this is not
a replacement for the protected repeat-3 acceptance gate.

### Full removal cost and larger opportunities

A and E are the first targets for a follow-up cleanup. Their selected tests total
539 lines, with A's additional 118-line analyzer: 657 function/helper lines before
imports and spacing. E's real harness support remains necessary. These are scope
estimates, not a promise that every assertion can disappear without choosing what
lost structural coverage to accept.

B's three fixture helpers remain used. C's source-scan helper and subject builder
could disappear only with wholesale cohort deletion, which the duplicate fault
rejects; preserving behavior will preserve some support. D's palette provenance,
raw-color scanner and shared token parsing remain. Selecting test functions alone
therefore overstates the ease of deleting support and understates the migration
work needed to preserve useful assertions. Exact cohort membership and source
spans are in the evidence manifest; there is no claim of a fully edited patch.

The largest single architectural choice is F: its self-test file has 2,594 lines,
the audit has 8,602 Python lines, and its reference JSON has 63,237 lines. Together
these are 74,433 lines, about 32.5% of the counted corpus; only 11,196 are Python.
Removing 95 cases alone leaves most of that machinery intact. Retiring the whole
system would require accepting the loss of independent frozen policy authority,
retaining necessary settlement regressions in ordinary tests, updating references
and explicitly changing AGENTS/EXECUTOR/DEFENSE protection. The prior rejected
TS-R6 extraction and this one-fault probe do not establish that replacement yet.
Those 11,196 Python lines are about 7.3% of the 153,650-line Python corpus.
The study establishes no large safe deletion; reference-data reduction must not
be confused with equivalent maintenance savings.
A less costly intermediate option is to keep semantic policy checks while choosing
whether every normalized trace field must remain frozen; that too changes evidence
policy and has no quantified saving in this study.

Prioritize maintenance freedom rather than a case-count target: delete redundant
source prescriptions; give each observable obligation one primary test owner;
keep focused behavioral checks for distinct failure modes; discuss retirement of
certification obligations separately. Do not collapse tests merely because their
names, fixtures or passing paths resemble each other. No permanent production,
test, oracle, or normative-policy change is part of this study.

### Final review refinements

B's separately manifested discriminator retains the selected target identity while
excluding only its source. Correct code: one pass. Deleted source filter: one
failure, an extra SOURCE-0101.BIN result. This confirms masking in the original
fixture, not a newly discovered production defect. Evidence and exact launcher:
`b-discriminator-01/results.json` and its adjacent manifest/script.

E is not purely lexical: preserve the selected materials test's pre-readiness
_Recorder.failure → EvidenceReader privacy check. The retained post-ready test
covers a different branch. The gallery media test also checks actual mapping
values. Conditional full source closure is approximately 427 lines plus separators:
412 selected lines, eight lines of _declared_keys, a one-line _SCRIPT constant,
and six import bindings. No actual harness, child, fixture, report parser or
scenario script becomes orphaned. Recommended deletion is smaller after splitting
and retaining those behavioral portions.

D's conditional full source closure is 1,333 lines plus separators: 1,187 selected
lines, the remainder of the 96-line motion module (67 selected lines already
counted), and 117 unused constant lines. Its department ownership entry also needs
removal. All six design-token helpers remain. Lost obligations include authored
palette and filter states, semantic aliases, forced-colors mappings, non-color
cues, stylesheet ordering and motion rules. These are not all dispensable spelling
checks. This cost is an upper bound for the ablated cohort, not a safe saving.

The first combined ordinary run is retained in runs-02: 4,456 passed, four
capability skips, 20 failures and 28 setup errors. The registered base interpreter
with dependency PYTHONPATH does not satisfy custody's active-venv origin checks
or GUI tooling's editable-project venv requirement. These failures do not count
as ablation evidence. A study-only venv and editable installation were created
inside the isolated control snapshot for a separately numbered calibrated run;
live environment files remain unchanged. The 36 neighborhood runs did not exercise
these full-suite setup requirements.

The observed constraints translate into concrete future work: a new failure
boundary requires owner-inventory edits; alternative batching hits surviving query
counts; index redesign hits representation requirements; new motion implementation
hits lexical bans; harness scenario evolution hits exact matrices; expanded audit
reports hit frozen authority. Engineering time saved by removing these constraints
was not measured. The larger opportunity is reducing duplicated obligations, not
promising a particular percentage of fewer test cases.

F's retirement closure also includes executable department ownership and active
references in EXECUTOR, TOOLS and M1_SHELL_H2, alongside the normative AGENTS and
DEFENSE decisions. Historical obsolete documents remain historical. No other
Python test imports the audit directly in the finite tracked corpus searched.

The calibrated runs-03 combined ablation (unchanged production) produced 4,503
passes, four capability skips and one failure, with no setup errors. The remaining
holdout check requires historical Git commits, absent from a source-only archive.
Local baseline history was then supplied to that disposable copy for runs-04.
Neither setup failure was attributed to test removal, and neither prompted a
change to protected tests. Combined ablation is not a paired intact full-suite
comparison; intact baseline gates remain the separately identified TS-R9 evidence.

Live production, tests, tools, AGENTS, DEFENSE and EXECUTOR match cab2259 by Git
diff. source-reconciliation.json also records raw SHA-256 hashes over that tracked
corpus. The report is this task's only live repository change; concurrent dirty
documentation belongs to the separate documentation study. No commit is made from
those shared files.

Final combined ablation, unchanged production (runs-04): **4,504 passed, four
known capability skips, 166 deselected** (138 cohort cases plus 28 headed cases).
The removed cohorts therefore coexist without an ordinary-suite interaction
failure in this configured snapshot. This green run does not reverse the observed
unique losses or the untested obligations above.

Combined-drop fault neighborhoods retained the same outcomes: A 24 failures,
B zero (the original masked fixture), C zero, D zero, E two, F two, with no setup
errors. D's retained neighborhood shrank from 60 to 58 cases because two E guards
also live in its gallery module. The combined result does not rely on a detector
that another selected cohort removed. B's separate discriminator remains necessary
to interpret its zero correctly.

Closeout: TA-1 through TA-3 are complete as a diagnostic study. Evidence comprises
47 pytest invocations (including both rejected combined setup runs) and three
real oracle commands. Nine temporary Python drivers/plugins were retired into a
byte-verified ZIP with per-source SHA-256 manifest; exact manifests, snapshots,
logs and replay notes remain ignored evidence. No study framework was added to
pytest. Recommended implementation is a subsequent decision, not started here.

for this pass. The three oracle options remain available for a later decision;
this rebase neither weakens it nor claims a compact equivalent replacement.
